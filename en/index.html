01Application and publisher

### DaliX Privacy Policy

02Provider policies

Dalix is an Android everyday-tools and services application published by PharaohStream, package com.daleel.egypt. No Dalix account is required. It is independent and non-governmental. Government-service information and links are informational and do not imply government endorsement.

03Local processing

Financial, household, informational health, conversion and date calculations run on your device. Supported preferences, favorites, reminders, prayer settings and reading records are stored locally according to your use. Dalix does not operate a server to synchronize those personal records. Feature code does not add calculation amounts, search text or reminder content to ad requests or custom Analytics events. This does not eliminate providers' processing of the technical information described below.

04Online services

Weather requests send your selected city and request parameters to WeatherAPI. Currency features retrieve public Frankfurter rates using its CBE source without sending your conversion amount. When you stream or download Quran audio, the chosen recording is requested over HTTPS from MP3Quran; the provider can receive your connection address and the requested recording. Supported downloads, playback position, reading plans and alert preferences remain on-device. External services receive ordinary connection information, including IP addresses.

05Usage analytics

Dalix uses Google Analytics for Firebase to measure usage and improve the application. Events can include screen names, feature identifiers, country pack, calculator mode/completion, search length, result count and selected result type, together with session, device and installation information processed by the service. Feature identifiers can reveal the section used, including Islamic features; we do not ask you to declare a religious belief. Custom fields do not contain salary or health values, coordinates or the search text itself.

06Crashes and performance

Firebase Crashlytics helps diagnose failures. Reports may include error messages, stack traces, device/OS information, app version and technical context such as language, theme and country pack, and preceding events useful for diagnosis. Processing depends on the service's settings and policies; this does not mean that all your local records are uploaded to Dalix.

07Advertising providers

Dalix uses Google AdMob and Google Mobile Ads; AppLovin may supply advertising through mediation. Providers may process device/advertising identifiers, device/network information, IP addresses and inferred general region, app/ad interactions and diagnostics for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Processing and personalization depend on region, choices, provider settings and device controls. An optional rewarded advertisement provides a period without full-screen ads as described in the app; eligible inline advertisements may remain.

08Consent and controls

Dalix refreshes advertising status through Google UMP, presents a form when required by the service configuration, and starts app-initiated ad requests only when that status permits them. Advertising privacy options appear in Settings when UMP requires them. They are not a universal switch for all processing and do not necessarily disable Firebase usage analytics or crash reporting. Android also provides advertising-ID and permission controls. Permission to request an ad must not be assumed to mean consent to every purpose or personalization.

09Local operational counters

During operation, the app may keep limited counts of ad loading, rendering and failures. They stay in process memory, without uploading or file persistence, and contain no input contents, revenue values or new user identifier. They clear when advertising request permission is removed and disappear when the process ends.

10Permissions and sharing

Camera access supports scanning you initiate; decoding happens on-device. Location is optional for associated tools and Android can permit approximate or precise foreground access; weather does not automatically request GPS. Notifications, alerts and audio services support enabled features; their contents may appear on your lock screen according to device settings. A sound file you select for an alert is processed locally. Opening links, emailing, calling or sharing transfers control to the application or recipient you choose and their policies apply.

11Retention, security and deletion

Local storage relies on Android protection; we do not claim a separately encrypted document vault. Direct feature requests use HTTPS; providers have their own security practices. Local data remains until edited, deleted or app data is cleared, subject to Android backup, restore and device transfer. Deletion does not automatically erase earlier backups, emails or shared files. There is no Dalix account or personal-record synchronization system. Providers retain their data under their policies and settings; we do not promise a universal period or immediate deletion of third-party records.

12Current Analytics retention settings

Settings reviewed on 22 November 2026 showed two months for event data and 14 months for user data, with reset on new user activity enabled. These periods do not cover all aggregate reports, Crashlytics records or every advertising provider and do not mean that all data is deleted after one universal period. Settings may change; deletion rights and processes depend on each provider, its configuration and applicable law.

13Age, rights and contact

The declared Google Play audience includes ages13 and above, not under13 groups; definitions of children and applicable safeguards vary by country. The current version has no user account or age-selection screen. Applicable law may provide access, correction, deletion, objection or consent-withdrawal rights. We cannot remotely access your local database; we can help identify the relevant device or provider controls. Contact Muhammed2del.ahmed@gmail.com, supplying only what is necessary for your request.

14Provider policies

Policy pages use GitHub Pages, whose host may process website visit information. See Google Privacy, Firebase Privacy, AppLovin Privacy, WeatherAPI, Frankfurter, MP3Quran and GitHub Privacy. The effective date will be set when the approved policy is published.
