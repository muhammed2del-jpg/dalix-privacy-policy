01Application and publisher

Dalix is an Android everyday-tools and services application published by PharaohStream, package com.daleel.egypt. No Dalix account is required. It is independent and non-governmental. Government-service information and links are informational and do not imply government endorsement.

02Local processing

Financial, household, informational health, conversion and date calculations run on your device. Supported preferences, favorites, reminders, prayer settings and reading records are stored locally according to your use. Dalix does not operate a server to synchronize those personal records. Feature code does not add calculation amounts, search text or reminder content to ad requests or custom Analytics events. This does not eliminate providers' processing of the technical information described below.

03Online services

Weather requests send your selected city and request parameters to WeatherAPI. Currency features retrieve public Frankfurter rates using its CBE source without sending your conversion amount. When you stream or download Quran audio, the chosen recording is requested over HTTPS from MP3Quran; the provider can receive your connection address and the requested recording. Supported downloads, playback position, reading plans and alert preferences remain on-device. External services receive ordinary connection information, including IP addresses.

04Usage analytics

Google Analytics for Firebase collection is disabled by default until eligibility is established. It is not enabled for users under 18 or with an unspecified age category. For adults, collection requires the usage-sharing choice and publication of the matching disclosures. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland it also requires appropriate Analytics consent, including analytics_storage; UMP permission to request ads is not enough. Collection stays off if the region or required consent cannot be verified. An independent Privacy setting allows users to disable or choose Analytics again, and disabling overrides earlier consent. Permitted events may include screen names, feature identifiers, country pack, calculator mode/completion, search length, result count and selected result type, together with session, device and installation information processed by the provider. Feature identifiers may reveal use of Islamic features; we do not ask users to declare a religious belief. Custom fields do not include salary amounts, health measurements, coordinates or search text. We do not add duplicate advertising-revenue events.

05Crashes and performance

Firebase Crashlytics is off by default for everyone. Sending reports requires an adult user to explicitly choose crash reporting, independently of Analytics and advertising consent. Reports may contain error messages, stack traces, device/OS information, app version, language, theme, country pack, Crashlytics/Firebase installation identifiers and session information when collected under SDK settings. Automatic uploading remains disabled; reports from a fully consented session may be sent on a later eligible launch. A new grant does not authorize reports from an earlier unconsented period. Switching off prevents new report-send requests, invalidates eligibility of pending reports and requests SDK deletion of unsent local reports when safe SDK initialization is available. A previously initialized SDK may retain local crash information until processing or restart; a later launch may be needed to complete disabling. Reports already sent cannot be recalled, and we do not promise immediate deletion of all provider records.

06Advertising providers

Dalix uses Google AdMob and Google Mobile Ads; AppLovin may provide advertising through mediation. The app does not start new ad requests for users under 18 or with an unspecified age category. For eligible adults, providers may process device/advertising identifiers, device/network information, IP addresses and inferred general region, app/ad interactions and diagnostics for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Processing and personalization depend on region, choices, provider settings and device controls. The local age choice is not inferred from these identifiers. Rewarded advertising is optional and provides a period without full-screen ads as described in the app; eligible inline advertisements may remain.

07Consent and controls

Privacy settings include a local age category without a date of birth and two independent controls: Share usage data to improve the app, and Send crash reports to improve stability. Disabling them or leaving age unspecified does not restrict core tools. Eligibility is reevaluated at startup and after choice or consent changes. Dalix refreshes advertising status through Google UMP for adults only, shows a form when required by the service configuration and starts app-initiated ad requests only when permitted. Advertising privacy options appear when UMP requires them; they do not imply consent to Analytics or Crashlytics. Android also provides advertising-ID and permission controls. Firebase sharing cannot be activated before matching disclosures are approved and published; eligibility, user choices and applicable consent remain necessary after publication.

08Local operational counters

During operation, the app may keep limited counts of ad loading, rendering and failures. They stay in process memory, without uploading or file persistence, and contain no input contents, revenue values or new user identifier. They clear when advertising request permission is removed and disappear when the process ends.

09Permissions and sharing

Camera access supports scanning you initiate; decoding happens on-device. Location is optional for associated tools and Android can permit approximate or precise foreground access; weather does not automatically request GPS. Notifications, alerts and audio services support enabled features; their contents may appear on your lock screen according to device settings. A sound file you select for an alert is processed locally. Opening links, emailing, calling or sharing transfers control to the application or recipient you choose and their policies apply.

10Retention, security and deletion

Local storage relies on Android protection; we do not claim a separately encrypted document vault. Direct feature requests use HTTPS; providers have their own security practices. Local data remains until edited, deleted or app data is cleared, subject to Android backup, restore and device transfer. Deletion does not automatically erase earlier backups, emails or shared files. There is no Dalix account or personal-record synchronization system. Providers retain their data under their policies and settings; we do not promise a universal period or immediate deletion of third-party records.

11Current Analytics retention settings

Settings reviewed on 22 November 2026 showed two months for event data and 14 months for user data, with reset on new user activity enabled. These periods do not cover all aggregate reports, Crashlytics records or every advertising provider and do not mean that all data is deleted after one universal period. Settings may change; deletion rights and processes depend on each provider, its configuration and applicable law.

12Age, rights and contact

The declared Google Play audience includes ages 13 and above; the selected audience groups have not been changed. Definitions of children and applicable requirements vary by country. The age and privacy controls described here apply to the update that includes them; earlier versions may not offer the same controls, so updating is needed to obtain them. We do not collect a date of birth or infer age from identifiers or behavior. Applicable law may provide access, correction, deletion, objection or consent-withdrawal rights. We cannot remotely access your local database; we can help identify the relevant device or provider controls. The publisher is PharaohStream. Privacy requests are received at Muhammed2del.ahmed@gmail.com; provide only information necessary for your request.

Policy pages use GitHub Pages, whose host may process website visit information. See Google Privacy, Firebase Privacy, AppLovin Privacy, WeatherAPI, Frankfurter, MP3Quran and GitHub Privacy. The effective date will be set when the approved policy is published.
