01Application and publisher

Dalix is an everyday-services Android app published by PharaohStream. Its production package is com.daleel.egypt. This policy explains its local features, external services and advertising. No Dalix account is required.

02Scope

This policy covers Dalix and this privacy website. It distinguishes local processing from external processing. Google, WeatherAPI, your device platform and websites you choose to open have their own policies. This policy is not a worldwide legal-compliance certification.

03Information on your device

Dalix processes calculation inputs, preferences, document metadata, commitments, payments, reminder schedules, prayer settings and public-data caches. Saving depends on your actions and the feature. Dalix does not operate a personal-data backend, user accounts or cloud synchronization.

04Calculations and sensitive inputs

Financial, household, date, conversion and informational health calculations run locally. Currency conversion amounts are not sent to the rate provider. Dalix feature code does not add financial or health inputs to ad requests. Avoid unnecessary sensitive inputs; calculations are not professional advice.

05My Files and commitments

My Files locally stores document type, expiry date and optional name or note; it is not a cloud document vault. Commitments can contain titles, categories, amounts, due dates, recurring plans and payment history. Avoid placing identification numbers or sensitive document contents in free-text fields.

06External services

Weather requests go directly to WeatherAPI.com. Rates come from Frankfurter using its CBE source. Advertising and consent use Google Mobile Ads and Google UMP. Providers receive network information needed to handle requests, including IP addresses. Local personal records are not uploaded to a Dalix backend.

07Google AdMob advertising

Google Mobile Ads can collect or share IP addresses, device/advertising identifiers, app/ad interactions, diagnostics and performance information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Personalized ads are not always enabled: consent, region, account configuration and device choices affect processing. See Google Privacy and Google’s SDK disclosure.

08Consent and advertising choices

Dalix updates consent information through UMP, presents a form when required and gates ad initialization on permission to request ads. Settings exposes advertising privacy options when UMP requires them; this is not a universal opt-out switch. Android also provides advertising controls. Regional messages depend on publisher configuration in Google’s services.

09Permissions

Camera permission supports scanning you start. Approximate location is optional and user initiated. Notifications support enabled reminders and prayer alerts. Internet/network access supports live services and ads; reboot handling restores enabled schedules. The app declares Android’s advertising-ID permission. Manage runtime permissions in Android settings; denial may limit the related feature.

10QR, camera and selected images

Generation and barcode decoding take place on-device. Scanning starts after your action; gallery reading uses an explicitly selected system-picker image. Images are not uploaded to a remote decoder. Dalix does not maintain scan history and deletes its owned temporary image copy after processing. Decoded links, calls and messages never open automatically.

11Location and prayer tools

Prayer and direction calculations use coordinates/settings you select or enter. A separate location tool can request foreground approximate location after your action; weather does not automatically use GPS. Selected settings can remain locally. Advertising providers may independently estimate a general area from IP information.

12Weather and currency requests

Weather requests include the selected city and forecast parameters. Currency requests retrieve public CBE rates through Frankfurter, not your conversion amount or payment history. Providers can process request/network metadata. See WeatherAPI Privacy and Frankfurter. Dalix displays sources/timestamps; cached data may be stale or unavailable.

13Reminders and notifications

Definitions, recurrence and snooze state are stored locally and use Android scheduling/notifications. Prayer alerts use saved settings. Notification text may appear on lock screens or connected devices according to Android settings; adjust visibility if it could reveal personal information. These are local alerts, not Dalix marketing messages.

14Device tools and explicit sharing

The phone toolbox reads device/Android version, screen, battery, storage and network status for local display, separately from ad SDK diagnostics. Copy actions put content in the system clipboard, potentially accessible to other permitted apps. External links and emails use their respective services. Do not assume copied or emailed information remains inside Dalix.

15Local storage, backup and deletion

Local records remain until edited/deleted or app data is cleared. Uninstall normally removes app-local data, subject to Android backup/restore. Dalix currently has no own cloud-backup or general export/import service. Android/device-transfer settings may back up or restore app data; manage those separately. Deleting a record does not erase existing backups, clipboard copies or emails. Clearing app data can permanently lose records. Records from the earlier native Android app are not imported into rebuilt Dalix: old files may remain on the device but are not accessible through the new interface.

16Security and retention limits

Live-provider requests use HTTPS; Google describes encrypted transmission for its ads SDK. Local storage relies on Android protections, not a separately encrypted document vault or a promise of perfect security. Public caches expire by feature. Providers, hosting and email services set their own retention; we do not claim fixed deletion periods for their records.

17Privacy rights and requests

Applicable law may grant access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, consent withdrawal or regulator-complaint rights, depending on your location and processing. Contact us for help. We cannot remotely access your local Dalix database; manage it on-device. Use providers’ controls for their records. Send only information necessary for your request.

18International use and children

PharaohStream intends Dalix to be suitable for broad age groups, but the current Google Play target-audience declaration selects ages 13–15, 16–17 and 18+, not under-13 groups. This is distinct from a content rating. Some users may still be children under local law. The app has no account or age-selection screen. Parents and guardians should supervise use and review device/ad choices. Contact us about inappropriate processing of a child’s information. Child-specific and regional advertising/consent safeguards must be verified before expanding distribution; this policy does not certify Families or worldwide compliance.

19This website and policy changes

This static site uses GitHub Pages, with no Dalix ads, analytics scripts, tracking cookies, forms or external fonts. GitHub can process visitor requests, including IP/browser metadata, under its privacy statement. Reading needs no login or JavaScript. We may update the policy when practices change; dates appear above. Arabic and English convey the same policy.
